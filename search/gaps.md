## Rewritten Row (in my own words)

**Original gap:** Quantified business-impact metrics in case study bullets

**My version:** My resume bullets right now just describe what I did (redesigned flows, improved findability) but don't say how much it actually helped — and recruiters clearly expect real before/after numbers, not just process descriptions. The good news is I'm not starting from zero here: for both MOKAHire and Ark7, I know there's usage/engagement data sitting with those teams that I never went back and requested after I left. My plan isn't "wait for a new project" — it's reach out to my former MOKAHire and Ark7 contacts specifically to ask what changed after my redesigns shipped (task completion time, engagement, conversion, whatever they tracked), and only put a number in resume.json if someone there can actually confirm it. If they can't give me anything concrete, I'll say so in the case study instead of guessing.
